- How to compile and run training

-- Compile (from snakesV2/):
    (test):
        g++ -std=c++17 -DNDEBUG -O3 test.cpp -o test
    (train):
        g++.exe -std=c++17 -DNDEBUG -O3 -pthread main.cpp -o train
    (visualize):
        g++ -std=c++17 -DNDEBUG -O3 visualize_layer.cpp -o visualize_layer

-- Run (from snakesV2/):
    (test):
        ./test <model_path_relative_to_snakesV2/> [speed_ms] [board_sz] <show?(0:1)>
    (from snakesV2/):
        ./train <ARGS> <ARGS> <ARGS> <ARGS>
    NOTE: V2 nets are board-size locked (input = board_size^2). Test with the
    same BOARD_SIZE the net was trained on. V1 24-ray nets cannot load on V2.


- What you'll see: one line per generation, e.g.:

$ > Gen 30 | Best Fitness: -7e-05 | Curr Fitness: -0.00022 | Best Score: 1 | Curr Score: 1
$ > Saving...
$ > Saved: trained_networks/run_1


- Every <N> gens (batch_save) it saves to 
    trained_networks/run_<N>/: best.simple_net
        , current.simple_net (for resume/early-stop)
        , and performance.txt (fitness, gen, score, game-over screenshots of best and current).
